**eventually consistent** is a briefing for cloud and AI architects and engineers. It covers two topics, each with its own schedule, archive and feed. Issues are published in the morning, US Eastern time.

## Cloud Digest: Mondays and Thursdays

- **Provider announcements**: new services, pricing changes, retirements, new regions and major previews from AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud and other clouds, taken from the providers' own pages.
- **Industry news**: earnings, deals, outages, regulation, AI infrastructure capacity and cloud-native releases.
- **Architecture & design patterns**: new papers and engineering write-ups on distributed systems, serverless, multi-cloud, platform engineering, resilience, FinOps, cloud security and architectures for AI workloads, with a note on where each applies.
- **Takeaway**: the main theme across the issue.

Archive: [/cloud/](/cloud/)

## AI Digest: Tuesdays and Fridays

- **Model & platform announcements**: new models, API and pricing changes, and model retirements from the major AI labs and model platforms, taken from their own pages.
- **Industry & policy news**: deals, funding, regulation, safety and security incidents.
- **Engineering patterns & research**: papers and engineering write-ups on building with AI, such as agents, retrieval, evaluation, inference cost and LLM security.
- **Takeaway**: the main theme across the issue.

Archive: [/ai/](/ai/)
