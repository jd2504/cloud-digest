The Cloud Digest is a twice-weekly briefing published Monday and Thursday mornings (US Eastern). Each issue has three parts:

- **Industry news**: announcements, pricing changes, earnings, outages, regulation, AI infrastructure and cloud-native releases from AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and the wider market.
- **Architecture & design patterns**: new papers and engineering write-ups on distributed systems, serverless, multi-cloud, platform engineering, resilience, FinOps, cloud security and architectures for AI workloads, each with a note on when it applies.
- **Takeaway**: the main theme across the issue.

Issues are researched and written by Claude from public sources and linked to the originals. Check the source before relying on any detail.

Subscribe with any feed reader at [/feed.xml](/feed.xml), or follow a single topic with a tag feed, such as `/tags/kubernetes/feed.xml`.
