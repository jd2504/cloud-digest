## Architecture & design patterns

### MoEless: serverless serving for mixture-of-experts models
Source: arXiv:2603.06350 (March 2026, cs.DC). Included as newly notable research, not from this window. The paper serves MoE language models by running experts as serverless functions, so hot experts scale out independently and load imbalance across experts drops. This trades some invocation overhead for better utilization. Apply it when expert popularity is skewed and traffic is bursty. [arXiv:2603.06350](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06350)

### C2CServe: fast model switching for serverless LLM inference
Source: arXiv:2605.19481 (May 2026, cs.OS). Also older than the window. It lets GPU partitions switch between models without reloading weights into GPU memory, with authors reporting up to 7.1x lower cold-start latency for dense models on GH200. Apply it when many low-traffic models share a GPU fleet and cold starts dominate latency. [arXiv:2605.19481](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19481)

### Embedding FinOps into the platform layer
Source: HCLTech trend commentary (not primary research). The argument is that cost controls such as tagging, forecasting and budget guardrails belong inside platform workflows, checked before deployment rather than in after-the-fact reports. A counterpoint essay warns that budget gates can override resilience choices like cross-region replication. Apply it when a platform team owns golden paths and wants cost feedback at deploy time, while keeping resilience requirements explicit. [HCLTech platform engineering trends 2026](https://www.hcltech.com/trends-and-insights/platform-engineering-trends-2026-foundation-ai-native-enterprise); [The platform team became a finance team](https://dev.to/ntctech/the-platform-team-became-a-finance-team-o6i)

## Takeaway

AI capacity is driving nearly every cloud story: record hyperscaler capex, a KubeCon agenda built around inference, and research that treats LLM serving as a serverless scheduling problem. The architectural pressure is to raise GPU utilization while keeping cost controls and resilience from fighting each other.
